About

I don't settle for the first answer.

I keep asking why until the right solution becomes clear.

I'm a Master of Professional Business Analysis graduate with experience across consulting, operations, property management, education and student services. My career started in hospitality and operations, where I learned to solve problems quickly, work with different people, and keep things moving when plans change. That experience led me to Business Analysis, where I found a way to combine practical instincts with structured thinking, analytical problem-solving and continuous improvement.

I'm naturally observant, curious, and quick to learn. Rather than accepting things as they are, I like to understand why a process exists, challenge assumptions, and explore whether there's a better way. I prefer understanding different perspectives before forming my own view, but once I have clarity, I'm confident leading discussions, making decisions, and guiding teams towards a shared outcome.

Outside the technical skills, I value ownership, integrity, adaptability and continuous improvement. I believe my strength lies in moving between the strategic and the practical — understanding the bigger picture while paying attention to the details that make things work.
